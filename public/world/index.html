This is an imagined Marvel crossover, built around detailed pixel-art landscapes and illustrated residents. The locations connect across one canvas. Residents repeat their illustrated puppet loops with signature effects. These are existing illustrations, not separately drawn action poses.

Drag to explore, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a resident to inspect their moment. Use Space to pause, F to fit a location, M for the whole map, / to search, or the arrow keys to pan.
